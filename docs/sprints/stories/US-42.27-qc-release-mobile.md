## Goal

Take the Mobile release through its four stages: the two beta builds get a full regression, the two production builds get a quick recheck that the right build is live and the wallet opens and works.

The version in the title is a placeholder. Fill in the real build number before closing the story, in the title and in `version_shipped`.

## What is in this release

Update web-runner to 1.3.90 ([SubWallet-Mobile #2057](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-Mobile/issues/2057)) — the whole programme QC'd in [US-42.24](US-42.24-qc-web-runner-1-3-90.md) and its sub-tasks, twenty-two web-runner versions from 1.3.71 to 1.3.90.

That programme checked each version's own items as they landed. This story is the release gate: the same build, on the stores, through the stages it actually ships in.

## Stages

| # | Stage | What runs there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | iOS beta — TestFlight | Full regression on a fresh install, then the upgrade recheck |
| 2 | Android beta — Google Play beta | Full regression on a fresh install, then the upgrade recheck |
| 3 | iOS production — App Store | Quick recheck |
| 4 | Android production — Google Play | Quick recheck |

A stage only starts when the one before it has passed. If a stage fails, the bug is logged and the release stops there.

## Before testing

- A TestFlight invite and the beta build installed on an iOS device.
- The Google Play beta track joined and the beta build installed on an Android device.
- The previous production version installed with accounts, balances, NFTs, staking positions and dApp connections on it, for the upgrade rechecks. Set this up before the beta build replaces it.
- Accounts and balances as the regression checklist needs them — the same test data [US-42.24.19](US-42.24.19-qc-web-runner-regression.md) used.
