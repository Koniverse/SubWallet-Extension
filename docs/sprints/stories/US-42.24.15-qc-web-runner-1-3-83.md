## Goal

Check the earning terms and conditions screen, on Android and iOS, on a fresh install and on an upgrade.

## What is in this version

Earning terms and conditions ([#5007](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-Extension/issues/5007)).

The issue body is empty — just a title. So these checks come from what the build does, and the points come from the title. From the title, this is a terms screen the user has to accept before staking through Earning.

## Before testing

- An account with enough balance to start an earning position.
- The previous web-runner version installed, with accounts and data, for the upgrade runs.

- [x] AC-3: Declining or going back cancels the flow, and no transaction is sent — Android + iOS fresh
