## Where it sits

Multisig notification behaviour is the surface on top of
[US-18.2](US-18.2-pending-transaction-detection-and-approval.md)'s pending-transaction detection —
a signatory learns a transaction is waiting for them. US-18.2 is `done` at 1.3.74 and this is an
*improve* round on a shipped capability, which in this epic has previously been recorded as a row
in the delivering story's ledger rather than a new page: [#4963](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-Extension/issues/4963)
(*Improve Multisig account feature*, 1.3.77) is a row in
[US-18.1](US-18.1-multisig-account-creation-and-management.md).

**This one gets its own page instead, for one reason**: #4963 was recorded *after* it shipped, when
its diff said what it had changed. #5093 has no diff and no body. A row reading *"Improve multisig
notification — todo"* inside a `done` story would make that story's ledger claim work it does not
describe. **Placement is provisional** and this page should be folded into a US-18.2 row if the
eventual diff turns out to be a narrow fix.
