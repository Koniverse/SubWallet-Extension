## Goal

Check the periodic chain-list maintenance on the Extension: that the chains turned off are gone from the picker without taking anyone's assets with them, the new chains connect and work, the rewritten RPC lists hold, and the Bittensor subnet rename did not move anyone's balance.

## What is in this change

[ChainList #710](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-ChainList/issues/710) — Maintenance of chainlist, by tunghp2002, branch `koni/dev/issue-710`. It lands as [ChainList PR #713](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-ChainList/pull/713) (143 files, +1468/−329) and reaches the Extension through [PR #5096](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-Extension/pull/5096).

Four separate pieces of work, each with its own failure mode:

RPC lists rewritten on 69 chains. Every IBP endpoint was removed — `*.ibp.network` returns NXDOMAIN and `*.dotters.network` returns SERVFAIL — and replacements were added from Dwellir, OnFinality, PublicNode, LuckyFriday, Rotko, Gatotech and Helixstreet. The issue says the default (first) RPC of each chain is unchanged. References [#704](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-ChainList/issues/704) (Rotko) and [Extension #5010](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-Extension/pull/5010) (Gatotech, IBP removal).

60 chains set to `chainStatus: INACTIVE` — 29 mainnet, 31 testnet. The entries and their assets were kept so references do not break; only the status changed. Among them are chains people hold real balances on: Moonbeam, Moonriver, Manta Atlantic, Centrifuge, Interlay, Kintsugi, KILT, Crust, Zeitgeist.

24 chains newly added — 12 mainnet (XGRChain, Robinhood Chain, MegaETH, Plasma, Somnia, Arc, Sei EVM, Kaia, Cronos, Berachain, Ronin, Flow EVM) and 12 testnet. All EVM. References [#712](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-ChainList/issues/712) (XGRChain).

102 Bittensor subnets resynced with taostats — 85 names, 7 symbols, 21 priceIds, 86 icons. Slugs were deliberately left alone so users keep their enabled tokens, because balances are keyed by `metadata.netuid` rather than by slug.

## Why the risk sits where it does

Turning a chain `INACTIVE` is the dangerous half. A user holding GLMR on Moonbeam or MANTA on Manta Atlantic must not find the balance gone, the history blank, or the account unable to open — the chain is retired, not the asset record. The issue is explicit that entries and assets were kept for exactly this reason, so the check is that the Extension honours it.

The Bittensor rename is the subtle half. Symbols moved between netuids — `テ` went from 14 to 90, `モ` from 84 to 108 — so a rename that followed symbols rather than netuids would show one subnet's balance under another's name. The issue says slugs were kept to prevent this. A wrong result here looks plausible rather than broken, which is why AC-19 reads the figure against the chain rather than against the app.

Setting up balances across chains that are about to be turned off is the reason this is a 20.

## Open questions for the developer

These come from the issue's own follow-up list and from reading PR #5096. They are not test results.

PR #5096 is not in a shippable state. It pins `@subwallet/chain-list` to `0.2.132-beta.2-pr-713-7aa00e22` — a build of PR #713, not a release — in five files, and in the sixth it comments out `fetchLatestChainData()` in `chain-service/index.ts` with the author's own note "TODO: TEMP … Revert before commit". That call is what pulls the live chain data, so with it off the build reads only the bundled chain-list. QC on this build therefore tests the bundled data, not the update path. Ask for a build with the release version and the fetch restored before AC-14 to AC-17 can mean anything.

`validate-tokens` crashes on `bittensor-LOCAL-Ё` (netuid 111, `assetType: null`), and the issue says every asset after it goes unvalidated. So the Bittensor half has not been fully checked by the tooling either.

Seven Bittensor slugs no longer match their symbol. The issue states this is deliberate — migrating slugs is unsafe because symbols moved between netuids — but it means `validate-tokens` reports them and will keep doing so.

Four chains marked `INACTIVE` have working RPCs again — `altair`, `core`, `boolAlpha`, `abstract_testnet` — and the issue lists re-enabling them as open. Worth asking whether they should be in this release or the next.

Two chains are still producing blocks but have no public RPC — Joystream and ChainX. They are `INACTIVE` for lack of an endpoint, not because they shut down. A user holding assets there should be told which case they are in.

Four replacement testnets are not added yet — X Layer testnet 1952, Celo Sepolia 11142220, Sonic testnet 14601, B² testnet 1163 — while their predecessors are being turned off in this change. Users of those testnets lose the network with nothing to move to.
