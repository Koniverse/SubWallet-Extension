## Before testing

- Nothing to set up on chain for most of it — this is mostly a display and connectivity pass.
- An account holding ARIAIP and one holding cACU, to check those two can now be sent.
- Balance on Xode and on Polkadot, for the two XCM checks.
- An account that held Phala before, to see what the removal looks like on an upgrade.
- The previous web-runner version installed, with accounts and data, for the upgrade runs.
