## Test notes

- Tested 2026-09-03 to 2026-09-05 — Mobile, Android and iOS, fresh install and upgrade
- Passed: AC-1 to AC-6 (Acurast, Monad, Stable, TRAC, vMANTA, xAlpha), AC-8 and AC-9 (ARIAIP and cACU transfers), AC-11 (Polkadot to Manta Atlantic XCM), AC-12 (Phala removed), AC-13 (inactive RPCs removed), AC-14 to AC-17 (Avail logos, Aventus and Subtensor EVM explorer links, updated RPCs), AC-19 to AC-21 (the upgrade runs)
- Skipped: AC-7 — Mobile does not support the Cardano ecosystem, so NIGHT cannot be checked here
- Failed: AC-10 — BUG-42.24.2-04; AC-18 — BUG-42.24.2-05, since fixed and AC-18 rerun in [US-42.24.20](US-42.24.20-qc-web-runner-verify-bugs.md)
- Not run: none — every AC has a verdict
- Five bugs logged, all still open

Every AC has a result: eighteen pass, one skipped for an ecosystem Mobile does not support, and two failed with bugs logged. A fail with a bug behind it is a conclusion, so nothing here is left hanging.

AC-19 covers the upgrade run and reads "AC-1 to AC-18 pass on upgrade". The upgrade behaved the same as the fresh install, failures included — AC-10 and AC-18 fail there too, on the same two bugs, and nothing new turned up.

BUG-42.24.2-01 and -02 were hit early and cleared on their own before any AC was settled; they stay in the record because they are real and the developer will fix them. BUG-42.24.2-03, the missing price chart, is still there and no AC covers it.

Three of the five reach past this chain-list version: the toggle problem happened on every network, the missing chart on every token, and the XCM fee failure on every route. They may not belong to v0.2.122 at all.
