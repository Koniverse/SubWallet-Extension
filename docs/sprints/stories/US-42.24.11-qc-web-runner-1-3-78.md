## Goal

Check the eight items that came into the web-runner with 1.3.78, on Android and iOS, on a fresh install and on an upgrade.

Parent: [US-42.24](US-42.24-qc-web-runner-1-3-86.md).

## What is in this version

The biggest version in the list, and three of the items are Bittensor features that need real funds on more than one network.

- XCM destination fee ([#4278](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-Extension/issues/4278)) — the fee charged on the receiving chain is now shown, taken from the xcm-fee API, and every path that used to pad the amount to cover it was rechecked. The AC for this half were written on 2026-09-14 from the transfer QC checklist, not from the issue, whose body is a single line.
- Liquid staking bridge fee ([#4803](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-Extension/issues/4803)) — the bridge step in liquid staking used a rough estimate; it now calculates the real XCM fee from origin fee, destination fee and hop fee. Also fixes a case where an amount above the minimum was wrongly rejected.
- Disable all networks ([#4970](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-Extension/issues/4970)) — one switch on the Manage Networks screen turns every network off, instead of toggling each by hand.
- Alpha tokens on Subtensor EVM ([ChainList #670](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-ChainList/issues/670)) — the alpha tokens that already had an xAlpha counterpart from [ChainList #634](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-ChainList/issues/634) are now supported on the EVM side too.
- Alpha token transfer ([#4900](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-Extension/issues/4900)) — Bittensor alpha tokens can be sent through the normal transfer flow.
- TAO to Subtensor EVM bridge ([#4901](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-Extension/issues/4901)) — native TAO moves between the substrate side and the EVM side. The issue body is empty, so these checks come from the build.
- Bittensor on-chain swap ([#4899](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-Extension/issues/4899)) — swap native TAO for alpha, and alpha for alpha, using the swapStakeLimit pallet.
- chain-list stable v0.2.127.

## Before testing

- Real TAO, on both the substrate side and the Subtensor EVM side, to test the bridge in both directions.
- Alpha tokens on at least two different subnets, so alpha to alpha swaps can be tested.
- Balance on both ends of an XCM route.
- A liquid staking position that involves a bridge step.
- The previous web-runner version installed, with accounts and data, for the upgrade runs.

Gathering TAO and alpha across subnets is the reason this is a 13. Without those holdings, three of the eight items cannot be tested at all.

## Things to check

XCM destination fee ([#4278](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-Extension/issues/4278))

The issue body is one line — handle the destination fee from /xcm-fee and recheck every path that added an excess amount to cover it. The AC below come from the QC checklist for the transfer feature instead, because that is where the real scope is: the fee display touches single-chain transfers on every ecosystem, every bridge type, the confirmation screen and the figures behind it. Only the main cases are written here.

- [x] AC-1: The destination fee shows on the XCM confirmation screen before sending — Android + iOS fresh
- [x] AC-1a: A single-chain transfer still works on each ecosystem — EVM, Bitcoin, Cardano, TON and Substrate — with both a normal amount and transfer max, and nothing in the logic or UI changed — Android + iOS fresh
- [x] AC-1b: On EVM and Substrate the fee can be paid with the default fee, a custom fee, or a chosen token, and the network fee row shows the amount above and its value below — Android + iOS fresh
- [x] AC-1c: A cross-chain transfer works on each bridge type and shows both the network fee and the cross-chain fee: XCM substrate to substrate, XCM substrate to EVM, SnowBridge in both directions, Across bridge, AvailBridge in both directions, Polygon Bridge and PoS Bridge — Android + iOS fresh
- [x] AC-1d: The transfer confirmation screen shows what the design calls for — sender and recipient with logo, name, address and network; amount with its value; the fee rows with amount and value; and the Cancel and Approve buttons — Android + iOS fresh
- [x] AC-2: The amount that arrives matches what was quoted, once the destination fee is taken into account — Android + iOS fresh
- [x] AC-2a: The figures on the confirmation screen add up — the amount equals what was typed, the network fee equals the native token taken on the origin chain, and the cross-chain fee equals the destination fee in whichever token pays it — Android + iOS fresh
- [x] AC-2b: After a transfer goes through, the recipient balance rose by the amount sent less the destination fee, and the sender native balance fell by the amount plus the network fee — Android + iOS fresh
- [x] AC-2c: History shows the same figures as the confirmation screen did — Android + iOS fresh
- [x] AC-3: AC-1 to AC-2c pass on Android + iOS upgrade

Liquid staking bridge fee ([#4803](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-Extension/issues/4803))

The AC below were written on 2026-09-14 from the liquid staking QC checklist. The case that matters is staking DOT on Bifrost when the DOT sits on Polkadot Asset Hub instead: the app has to bridge it across first, so the available balance, the estimated fee and the amounts on each confirmation step all have to account for the XCM hop. Acala liquid staking follows the same logic.

- [x] AC-4: The estimated fee on Start earning is the mint fee alone when no XCM is needed, and mint fee plus XCM fee when DOT has to come from Polkadot Asset Hub — Android + iOS fresh
- [x] AC-4a: Available balance on Start earning shows what can really be staked — the DOT on Bifrost on its own, or Bifrost plus Asset Hub less the estimated fee when the hop is needed — Android + iOS fresh
- [x] AC-4b: The figures on the confirmation screen add up: with no hop the amount equals what was typed; with a hop the transfer step carries the amount plus the destination fee, and the mint step carries the amount typed — Android + iOS fresh
- [x] AC-5: An amount at or above the minimum transferable amount is accepted, not wrongly rejected — Android + iOS fresh
- [x] AC-5a: The Stake button is disabled with nothing entered, and letters, symbols and negative numbers cannot be typed — Android + iOS fresh
- [x] AC-5b: An amount within the balance opens the right confirmation — the stake confirmation with no hop, the transfer confirmation when a hop is needed — Android + iOS fresh
- [x] AC-5c: An amount above what is available opens the Insufficient balance popup, which names the maximum and where the balance sits — Android + iOS fresh
- [x] AC-5d: When the dry run fails, the message asks for a higher amount and names the minimum — Android + iOS fresh
- [x] AC-5e: The same checks hold for a QR account, where the confirmation steps are signed one at a time — Android + iOS fresh
- [x] AC-5f: Acala liquid staking behaves the same way as Bifrost — Android + iOS fresh
- [x] AC-6: AC-4 to AC-5f pass on Android + iOS upgrade
