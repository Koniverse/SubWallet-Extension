## Goal

Re-check the parts of the chain list that changed after [US-42.28](US-42.28-qc-chainlist-710-maintenance.md) closed, so the ticks in that story still mean something.

## Why this is a story of its own

US-42.28 closed on 2026-10-07 at all 306 lines. Two commits landed on the [ChainList #710](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-ChainList/issues/710) branch on 10-09, after that, and they touch chains the closed story already ticked.

Reopening a closed story would hide when each result was true. This one covers only what moved, and names the US-42.28 line each item invalidates.

## What changed on 10-09

`23aebcda2a` — a final re-check of all 455 RPCs across the 208 active chains. The developer retried failures twice more, resolved DNS through Cloudflare and Google, tested reachability from five other countries, and read para heads on the Kusama relay chain. 28 endpoints removed, 20 added.

`b671f28227` — two more Bittensor subnets resynced, with symbols checked against `SubtensorModule.TokenSymbol` on chain and priceIds against CoinGecko.

Both land in beta `0.2.132-beta.5`.

## Before testing

- The build carrying chain-list `0.2.132-beta.5` or later, not the beta.4 that US-42.28 was run against.
- A balance on `robonomics` or `bounceBitEvm` if one can be arranged, to check the deactivation does not take it.
- An account on GEB (formerly BEVM) with a small balance, for the rename and the explorer link.
