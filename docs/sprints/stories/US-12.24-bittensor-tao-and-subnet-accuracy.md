## Goal

Give [#5112](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-Extension/issues/5112) — *"Improve Bittensor TAO &
subnet support: on-chain yield, prices, staking and swap"* — a home in the docs
([D108](../../CONTEXT.md)): every TAO and subnet figure in Earning, Assets and Swap comes from the chain
and agrees across screens, and staking, unstaking, transfer and swap no longer fail or mislead.

This is the slice of the TAO & subnets tracker ([2026-10-12 §G](../../notes/2026-10-12.md#g-research--push-tao--subnets-bittensor))
that the tracker said would become its own EPIC-12 story once scheduled (rule 9). The tracker keeps the
detail and the evidence for every item; this story keeps the scope and the acceptance criteria.

## Why EPIC-12 owns it

By plurality, as with #4189: the yield figures, staking, unstaking, root claim and validator picker are
Earning. The swap rows (EXT-28, EXT-29, EXT-36) touch [US-11.7](US-11.7-in-wallet-bittensor-dtao-swap.md)'s
capability and the Assets and price rows (EXT-2, EXT-10, EXT-39, EXT-40) touch balances, but they ship in
the same PR, so one story carries them. [US-12.13](US-12.13-earning-reward-and-apy-accuracy-hardening.md)
stays the NFR-22 accuracy cluster for every pool type; this story is the Bittensor slice. The request-load
rows defend NFR-20, not NFR-22 (related: [US-20.2](US-20.2-api-call-optimization.md)).

## Scope

The issue's task list is the scope. It improves capabilities that already shipped —
[US-12.6](US-12.6-bittensor-dtao-subnet-staking.md) (dTAO subnet staking),
[US-12.23](US-12.23-bittensor-manual-claim-native-staking.md) (root claim) and
[US-11.7](US-11.7-in-wallet-bittensor-dtao-swap.md) (dTAO swap) — as new work in its own story (rule 9),
so those stay `done`. Order inside the PR follows the tracker: the P0 rows first — AC-1 to AC-7
(wrong numbers or blocked actions) — then the rest. Not in it, deferred by the owner: one symbol source per subnet
(EXT-13), Swap for Ledger accounts (EXT-30), and MEV Shield until after the maintenance PR's review.
EXT-32 (Subtensor-EVM alpha reads) lands with [PR #5001](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-Extension/pull/5001);
this story stays out of `evm.ts`, which #5001 rewrites.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **AC-1** — Yield (EXT-1): the pool card shows "Up to X% APR", the position detail shows the APR
  of the position's own validator(s), stake-weighted; a measured zero shows "0% APR", "—" only when
  there is no figure. Labelled *in alpha* for subnets and *in TAO, before the claim fee* for root. When
  BE has no subnet figure, the extension computes it from the chain.
- [ ] **AC-2** — Alpha → USD is valued on-chain (alpha → TAO × TAO/USD) in Assets, Earning and Swap,
  and one position shows the same alpha, TAO and USD value on all three (EXT-2, EXT-10).
- [ ] **AC-3** — Change validator shows no fee the move does not charge (EXT-25).
- [ ] **AC-4** — Unstake: a full exit has no minimum and "Unstake all" sends a full exit; a partial
  exit is checked against the chain minimum; minimums come from the chain, root included; a subnet whose
  price reads 0 can still be unstaked (EXT-26, EXT-21, EXT-20, EXT-27).
- [ ] **AC-5** — Swap TAO → alpha: the balance check, the quote and the submit all use root stake, and
  the swap is blocked when there is none (EXT-28); root hotkeys still under the root hold
  (`RootStakeUnlockInterval`) are skipped, and a missing or short root stake gets a clear message; a swap
  split across hotkeys never builds a leg below the chain minimum (EXT-36).
- [ ] **AC-6** — Root claim shows what a claim actually pays, uses the claim call that fits the
  account's validators, and notifies only when `redeemableTao` ≥ 2 × the claim fee (EXT-9).
- [ ] **AC-7** — The Assets total includes alpha staked through Earning (EXT-39); prices are requested
  in batches so no token is dropped, on every network (EXT-40).
- [ ] **AC-8** — Conversion: one alpha → TAO price scale (EXT-4); the stake / unstake preview comes
  from the chain's swap simulation, fee included, with no `fee_rate` request (EXT-5, BE-5); the limit
  price comes from the post-trade price and the user's slippage (EXT-6, EXT-29).
- [ ] **AC-9** — Conviction locks cap unstake, move and transfer to the unlocked amount (EXT-19).
- [ ] **AC-10** — Send alpha checks per-subnet transfer availability, the minimum and the
  recipient's lock conflicts (`LockHotkeyMismatch`, `AccountRejectsLockedAlpha`) before submitting, and
  warns about locked alpha (EXT-12).
- [ ] **AC-11** — The validator picker is per subnet, with per-subnet stake and take (EXT-14; needs BE-4).
- [ ] **AC-12** — Copy: subnet positions say rewards compound into stake (EXT-15); no hardcoded
  unstaking period or payout interval (EXT-16); "Emission" / "Max APY" and the new labels live in the
  locale files (EXT-18).
- [ ] **AC-13** — Unit tests cover price decoding, conversion, limits and minimums (EXT-17).
- [ ] **AC-14** — Ledger: stake, unstake, move, transfer and a root claim are signed on a real device
  and the run is recorded (EXT-24); the allowed-chain list has an offline fallback and proof metadata
  refreshes after a runtime upgrade, on every network (EXT-37, EXT-38).
- [ ] **AC-15** — Request load: one shared stake read; the cache timer stops on teardown; empty
  positions are emitted only on change; subnet metadata refreshes less often (EXT-31, EXT-33, EXT-34,
  EXT-35).
