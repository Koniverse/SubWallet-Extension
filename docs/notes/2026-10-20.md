## A. PR #5113 — the staking minimum follows the chain; BE-13 corrected

`66b6a1b259` *"Staking minimum follows the chain"*, 2026-10-19 11:51 UTC. The PR is now 16 commits,
+2,892 / −420 in 60 files; **CI green** on that head (*Build Development Preview*, Netlify, the Nextcloud
build and its Firefox build); still no review, body empty.

- **What it does (EXT-20, AC-4):** the smallest stake is the chain's own rule (`validate_add_stake`) —
  `DefaultMinStake`, 0.002 TAO, plus on a subnet the pool fee on a buy that size — read from the chain,
  instead of the raw `NominatorMinRequiredStake` taken as rao (0.01 TAO on root) or the hardcoded 0.021 TAO
  on subnets. Validation refuses below the exact minimum; pools, positions and validators show it rounded
  up to two digits (0.002 TAO on root, 0.0021 TAO on subnets). The root handler no longer reads the minimum
  from the pool cache or `yield-pools.json`. 1 test; the message is reworded and now translated in all
  locales (it was English before).
- **Testnet, as the commit records:** root 0.001999999 and SN3 0.002001006 TAO refused by both the
  extension and the chain (`AmountTooLow`); 0.002 / 0.002001007 / 0.0021 TAO taken by both.
- **Corrected in the tracker:** BE-13 proposed publishing **0.02 TAO** as root's `minValidate`, and the G.6
  *Minimums* row said the 0.01 TAO shown was "half the real one". 0.02 TAO is the minimum a partial unstake
  must leave (EXT-26); the staking minimum is 0.002 TAO (+ the pool fee on a subnet), as G.6's own text and
  this commit's testnet check show. Both struck through and corrected; EXT-20 annotated.
- **AC-4 now lacks only EXT-27 for unstake** (a subnet priced at 0) — no commit claims it yet.
