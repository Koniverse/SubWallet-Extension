## A. TAO & subnets tracker — root claim fee and claim shape

The tracker stays where it was written, [2026-10-12 §G](2026-10-12.md#g-research--push-tao--subnets-bittensor);
this section only records the pass. Measured on 2026-10-13 against spec 477, written today; the detail
is under EXT-9.

- **A claim costs ≈ 0.00825 TAO, flat.** `paymentInfo` and seven on-chain claims agree, whatever the
  claim redeems. 87 of 318 validator positions the extension counts as claimable would pay less than
  that.
- **`claim_root` is refused at admission, fee kept, for 55 of 112 coldkeys with two or more
  claimable validators** (of 120 multi-validator coldkeys sampled). The extension sends it whenever
  two or more validators are claimable; its 256-unit budget covers every root hotkey plus every
  basket row, and the 46 baskets that hold 99.9% of root NAV have 119–126 rows each (of the other
  144: 121 under 60, 11 at 60–118, 12 empty; 10-14). The sample came from stakers of the 12 baskets
  with the most shares, so "three or more validators go over" holds for large baskets only.
- **`claim_root_with_hotkey` always fits, but one per transaction** — its declared weight leaves no
  room for a second in a `batchAll`.
- **Changed in §G:** EXT-9 (P0) gains the measurements and the rule for choosing between the two
  calls; G.3 #12 extended and #14 added; G.6 *Root rewards* — "claim fee grows with holdings" struck,
  budgets added.

## B. TAO & subnets tracker — Assets vs Earning totals

Research item 3, read from `subwallet-dev` at `c5c9668715`; the detail is under EXT-11 and EXT-39 in
[2026-10-12 §G](2026-10-12.md#g-research--push-tao--subnets-bittensor).

- **Nothing is counted twice.** The only total is Assets', built from balances; root stake enters it
  once, as locked TAO. Earning feeds no total.
- **Alpha staked through Earning drops out of it (EXT-39, new, P0).** Balances are read only for
  visible tokens, and nothing turns a subnet's alpha token on when the account stakes into it — not
  the stake itself, not turning Bittensor on (the priority list holds only TAO), not the Subscan-based
  auto-enable. The total falls by what was staked while Earning shows the position. Derived from
  code; not reproduced in the UI.
- **Also left out:** alpha with no `priceId` (BE-7), unclaimed root rewards (EXT-9), SN111 on
  `0.2.131` builds.
- **Changed in §G:** EXT-11 ticked with the answer; EXT-39 added to P0; G.2 *Alpha balance*, G.3 #6
  and BE-7 updated.

## C. TAO & subnets tracker — limit price confirmed on mainnet

Research item 2. The question was whether G.3 #4's rule — the chain holds a limit against the pool
price **after** the trade, not the average fill — survives contact with real orders. It does.

- **Method:** every `add_stake_limit` / `remove_stake_limit` in blocks 9,277,913 – 9,279,981 (1,681
  orders), each replayed at its parent block with `sim_swap_*` and judged under both rules; orders
  sharing a block with another stake on the same subnet were dropped. The archive node dropped
  partway, so 399 were replayed.
- **Result:** of 351 fill-or-kill orders that executed or failed on price, the post-trade rule
  predicts all 351, the average-fill rule 345. The six where the rules disagree all followed the
  post-trade rule — among them two SN94 unstakes (2.3% and 2.5% of the pool) that failed
  `SlippageTooHigh` with the limit inside the average fill. A partial fill on SN90 stopped where the
  post-trade price met the limit.
- **Still derived at this point:** the UI's window and the swap case, EXT-29 — both measured later
  the same day (§D).
- **Changed in §G:** G.3 #4 (derived → ✔ chain), EXT-6, EXT-29, G.6 *Extrinsics*; a dated paragraph.

## D. TAO & subnets tracker — derived items measured with real transactions

The owner asked that the tracker rest on measurement, not inference. Everything a chain can settle
was run as a real transaction, built by the extension's **own** staking and swap code (verbatim from
`subwallet-dev` at `c5c9668715`), on two runtimes at spec 478 — a local node
(`subtensor-localnet:v478`) and testnet, with a testnet-only account (0.0296 testnet TAO spent, all
positions exited afterwards). The detail is the *Measured with real transactions* paragraph in
[§G](2026-10-12.md#g-research--push-tao--subnets-bittensor).

- **Confirmed:** the UI-allows / chain-refuses window (G.3 #4, EXT-6), the swap room below the setting
  (EXT-29: 0.3 – 0.4% usable of 0.5% set), a sub-minimum leg reverting the whole batch (EXT-36), a
  full exit of a position under 0.021 TAO (EXT-26), and a same-subnet move without a swap fee
  (EXT-25).
- **Corrected:** EXT-28's empty `batchAll` costs **no fee** — the defect is a swap reported as done
  while nothing moved; the unstake window starts at 0.30% of the pool, not 0.25%.
- **New:** an order refused on price still pays the network fee.
- **Could not be measured this way:** the React screens (the UI check is one comparison, from code),
  root claims (over budget on testnet), mainnet's fee for an empty batch. A Chopsticks fork of
  mainnet cannot run this runtime (missing BLS host function).
