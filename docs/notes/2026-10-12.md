### G.3 Findings worth acting on

1. **The subnet "Emission" number is sold as yearly yield.** The pool card labels `taoInEmission /
   10^7` honestly as *Emission*; two other screens do not — the instruction modal puts it into
   *"Earn up to {{apy}} yearly"*, and the **position detail** (`EarningInfoPart.tsx:82`) shows it as
   *"Estimated earnings X% per year"*. That is the NFR-22 failure exactly: a figure with no source for
   what it claims to be.
2. **Three alpha prices, one position.** Assets values alpha by CoinGecko; Earning values the same
   stake by the on-chain spot price; Swap uses the BE quote. They will disagree, and the user sees all
   three. Three mainnet subnets (SN83, SN95, SN101) have no CoinGecko id, and the 55 EVM / Base copies
   have none either although each maps to a mainnet `netuid`.
3. **The validator data is stale and root-only.** 24 days old on the CDN, and the subnet picker
   shows root validators with root-level stats — a validator active on a subnet but with no root
   stake is not offered there.
4. **The UI and the chain check slippage against different prices.** The UI's preview is
   constant-product on `taoIn/alphaIn` and gates on the **average execution** slippage ≤ the setting.
   The chain compares the limit with the **pool price after the trade** — `target_price` in
   `pallets/swap/src/pallet/swap_step.rs`, computed from the reserves after a full fill — and with
   `allow_partial = false` rejects anything past it. The post-trade price moves about twice as far as
   the average: at weights 0.5 a stake of δ of the pool's TAO moves it by (1 + δ)². So at the default
   0.5% setting, **a stake between about 0.25% and 0.49% of the pool's TAO passes the UI and is
   rejected on chain**; below that it executes, above it the UI already blocks it. An unstake has the
   same window in the pool's alpha. ~~*Derived, not observed:* the model matches `sim_swap_*` to about
   0.001% on SN64, SN66, SN82, SN85, but no rejected transaction has been seen.~~ **✔ chain (10-14):**
   replaying mainnet's own limit orders, the post-trade rule predicts **351 of 351** fill-or-kill
   outcomes, an average-price rule 345; the six cases where the two disagree all went the post-trade
   way — e.g. an unstake of 2.27% of SN94's alpha, limit 1.7% above the post-trade price and 0.45%
   below the average fill, failed `SlippageTooHigh`. **Window measured 10-14 with real transactions**
   built by the extension's own code, swept in 0.05%-of-pool steps on a v478 localnet. Stake: 0.20%
   executed, 0.25% refused. Unstake: 0.25% executed, 0.30% refused. The UI passed every step up to
   0.45% and blocked 0.50%. Those are sweep steps, not boundaries: from the chain rule the boundary
   is 0.2498% of the pool's TAO for a stake and 0.2510% of its alpha for an unstake (the 0.05% fee
   sits on the input), and from the code the UI's is δ / (1 + δ) + 0.0001 ≤ 0.005, about 0.49% — so
   the window is the same both ways, about 0.25 – 0.49%. *(Correction 10-14: an earlier line here
   read the 0.30% step as an unstake window "narrower than derived" — it is not.)* On testnet (spec
   478) a 0.30% stake and a 0.35% unstake both passed the UI check and failed `SlippageTooHigh` —
   and still paid the network fee (0.000851 TAO). The pool is also **not** plain `x·y=k` (G.6) — it
   only agrees today because the weights sit at 0.5.
5. **Root "Max APY" is the best validator's 30-day figure**, labelled as such on the card, but the
   same number is the instruction modal's *"Earn up to"* line and the position detail's *"Estimated
   earnings X% per year"* — where it reads as what this position earns.
6. **Alpha shows as `free` in Assets** although it is stake. ~~Whether Assets' total double-counts or
   misses anything against Earning's total has not been checked.~~ **Checked 10-14 (EXT-11):** nothing
   is counted twice, but the Assets total misses things — above all, alpha staked through Earning
   whose token was never made visible (EXT-39).
7. **No unit test** behind the I96F32 decode, the threshold fallback, or the price-scale division
   (carried from US-12.23).
8. **Swap TAO → alpha spends root stake but checks free TAO.** With no root stake it builds
   `batchAll([])` — ~~a no-op that still pays the fee~~ a no-op the chain reports as **successful**,
   at no fee (measured 10-14 on testnet; mainnet's `paymentInfo` is 0 too): the user is told the

**Root stake out, pending reward kept, 2026-10-14.** EXT-28's swap spends root stake, so does a
reward waiting to be claimed survive it? The source says yes: before root stake leaves, the
chain flushes the hotkey's queued dividends, then rebases the staker's claimed watermark by
rate × amount (v478 `claim_root.rs` `rebase_basket_claimed_for_stake_delta` — *"a stake change
never retroactively grants or destroys accrued claimable"*). Credits under the flush threshold stay
queued and go to whoever is staked when they flush. **Measured on mainnet, read-only**: every
`StakeRemoved` on netuid 0 in blocks 9,279,225 – 9,280,724 (230 coldkey–hotkey pairs), each read at
the parent block and at the block with `get_root_basket_claim_previews`, `BasketRate`,
`BasketClaimed` and the root stake. 178 had a pending reward, no claim in the same block and more
than 0.1% of their root stake removed — 43 swaps, 47 unstakes, 83 transfers, 5 moves; median 4.6%
removed, 23 full exits. **Owed shares were kept in all 178**, at worst 5 shares low (fixed-point
rounding), where a pro-rata loss would have cut them by the share removed. The 23 full exits still
show in the preview and stay claimable. The watermark moved by rate × amount to within
8.3 × 10⁻⁷. `redeemableTao` moved −0.6% to +0.13% with prices, shares unchanged. Not measured: the
sub-threshold credits. Also from the source: every move off root checks `RootStakeUnlockInterval`
(`RootStakeLocked`); it is 0 on mainnet and testnet today (EXT-28, G.6 *Unstaking*).
