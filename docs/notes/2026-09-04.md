## Worth keeping

- **`BUG-42.24.2-03` was a missing feature, not a regression** — `da0fbbcbae` is *"**feat**: add the
  token price chart"*. It was filed under US-42.24.2 (chain-list v0.2.122) and belonged to neither
  that version nor any other.
- **Most findings land outside the version that caught them.** `-02`, `-03` and items 4–9 are app-wide
  or first-run bugs, not chain-list ones — which is why 4–7 ended up on US-42.24.19 (regression)
  rather than on a version sub-task. A version-by-version backfill sweeps the whole app, so expect
  this to keep happening.
- **Merged checkboxes have now cost twice.** Item 2 held two bugs behind one checkbox and was ticked
  without being split, so which one it closed was unreadable; item 15 held `BUG-42.24.5-03` and
  `-04`, and because `-04` appears only in the collapsed body this note called it untracked four
  times. **A checkbox is a title plus a body, and only the body is complete.** Split merged items
  before ticking, or the record stops being reconstructable from the record.
