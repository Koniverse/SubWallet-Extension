**A reading that has not changed in five weeks stops being a status check and becomes evidence.**
Up to now these have been written down as *stalled* — a word that implies work paused and will
resume. Three identical readings say something narrower and more useful: **no work is in flight on
any of them.** Two of the six do have open PRs ([#5001](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-Extension/pull/5001)
for #4984, [#4996](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-Extension/pull/4996) for #4995), and
those PRs are themselves from June and May with no activity since.

### Why this matters more than the number

The 96 points are not the problem — nobody is counting on them. What the rows do is **make every
sprint closeout read wrong**. W36, W37 and W38 were each under-reported at closeout because the
scope table was trusted instead of the stories' own `sprint:` field; W38 missed 4 stories and 34
points. Twenty rows carrying a stale `sprint:` are twenty chances for the same mistake, and they
sit in the same field the closeout rule says to re-read.

A `ready` story in a closed window is also a claim in the sense of
[D107](../CONTEXT.md#d107-a-ticked-ac-is-a-claim-about-the-code--four-of-us-51s-were-false-and-one-was-a-p0-security-claim) — it says this was scoped and is waiting to start. For US-1.4 it
has been saying that since July.

### The call, and it is not mine to make

The honest end state is **`status: backlog` with `sprint:` cleared** on all twenty — that is what
*not scoped into any window* looks like, and it takes them out of every future closeout count
without deleting a single story or losing the anchors. The alternative, moving them into the
current window, would be recording work nobody is doing.

Not done here: twenty frontmatter edits are a planning decision with a board behind it, and this
note is not a mandate. **Recorded so the next closeout does not rediscover it.** Fourth window in a
row that this has been raised in.
