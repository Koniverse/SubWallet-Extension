## F. Monorepos — BE-14: the swap quote's fee from the submit's legs

[`10d7d69`](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-Monorepos/commit/10d7d69) *"Bittensor swap quote fee from the legs the submit sends"*, `tunghp2002`, 05:30 UTC, on `SubWallet-Monorepos` `koni/dev/issue-5112-extension` — off
`subwallet-backend-dev` / `-prod` at `99981a7`; ~~no PR yet~~ *(corrected 10-22: [PR #167](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-Monorepos/pull/167) was opened at 05:32 UTC, two minutes after the commit — the search missed it)*. +49 / −3 in three files:
`libs/swap-service/src/lib/services/bittensor/index.ts`, its new spec (3 tests), and
`apps/subwallet-backend/src/modules/swap/services/bittensor.service.ts`.

- **The fee:** a Bittensor quote's network fee is now estimated on the calls the submit sends — one `swapStakeLimit` per
  hotkey holding stake on the origin netuid, root stake for TAO (not the transferable balance), largest first. With no
  stake there, it is estimated on one leg for the whole amount: before, `batchAll([])` — a call that moves nothing — was
  quoted, at a fee of 0.
- **The amount:** zero is refused (`AMOUNT_CANNOT_BE_ZERO`). A stake short of the amount still gets its quote:
  *"refusing it is the client's check (EXT-28), as with every other provider"*.
- **Against BE-14's 10-14 wording** — *"the quote and its validation accept an amount up to the usable root stake"* —
  the quote is not capped; the cap lives in PR #5113's validation (`dce3b527e5`), which refuses an amount above the
  usable stake before submit. Recorded as built; the owner's call whether that settles BE-14.
- **To check:** the backend's legs take every hotkey with stake, largest first; the extension's plan
  (`bittensor-swap-plan.ts`) skips hotkeys under the leg minimum, on the root hold or locked, and submits the smallest
  first. With such hotkeys the two leg counts differ, so the quoted fee can be off by a leg's fee.
- **Not touched:** BE-15, BE-16, BE-17, BE-19 (the quote's impact and fee for ALPHA → TAO and ALPHA → ALPHA); BE-18 (one
  RPC, no fallback).
- **Changed:** 10-12 §G (BE-14 annotated), US-12.24 (BE table, AC-5), W43, CLAUDE.md.
