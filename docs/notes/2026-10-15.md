## A. TAO & subnets tracker — subnet yield from the chain (BE-2)

Research item BE-2, which blocks the full fix of EXT-1. Read-only on mainnet; the detail is the
*Subnet yield from the chain* paragraph in
[§G](2026-10-12.md#g-research--push-tao--subnets-bittensor).

- **The figure shown today is not a yield.** `taoInEmission / 10^7` reads 0.0% in the median subnet;
  stakers earn 27.5% APR in alpha over all staked alpha (p90 77%), and 53.9% with the best
  validator.
- **Formula:** r = D / A per epoch — D the nominators' dividend, take already out; A the hotkey's
  alpha just before the epoch — and APR = r × 2,629,800 / tempo. It matches real nominators' stake
  growth (129 / 131) and the v478 source, and holds from one epoch to the next (0.02% median).
- **Computable from current state:** D / (A_now − D) is 0.004% off at the median, so the extension
  can work out one subnet without an archive node.
- **Nothing official to reuse:** the runtime's `return_per_1000` predates dTAO; the SDK shows
  emission per day, not a return.
- **Decided (owner):**
  - pool card: "Up to X% APR", from the best validator holding ≥ 1% of the earning stake;
  - position detail: the APR of the position's own validator;
  - APR, labelled *in alpha*;
  - a subnet that paid stakers nothing shows "0% APR" with *"No rewards to stakers in the last epoch"*, never blank; "—" only when there is no figure (no epoch yet, `tempo` 0, or every read failed);
  - BE computes it each epoch from the chain, per subnet and per validator; the extension computes
    the subnet on screen when BE has nothing. For subnets this replaces the proxy's APR route
    (BE-20).
- **For the implementation** (written under BE-2 and EXT-1): D is already net of every take — do not subtract it again; a position spans every hotkey it holds on the subnet, so its APR is stake-weighted; parents earning through child keys have no permit but do earn; a hotkey with no entry earns 0; epochs can come early or late (owner trigger, per-block cap), though all 384 measured matched tempo; the figure is in alpha; root is not covered.
- **Changed in §G:** EXT-1, BE-2, BE-4, BE-20, G.2 *Subnet pool figure* and *Validator APY*, G.6
  *Subnet yield*, a dated paragraph.
