## A. Monorepos PR #167 — BE-19 and BE-18

Two commits on `koni/dev/issue-5112-extension` on 2026-10-22 (UTC). [PR #167](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-Monorepos/pull/167) is now 5 commits, +429 / −32 in six
files; no review, body empty, no CI checks. **Every swap-backend item left on #5112 — BE-14 to BE-19 — is on it.**

- **[`b51ac4c`](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-Monorepos/commit/b51ac4c) — BE-19, 10:05.** The chain refuses a move whose sale pays `DefaultMinStake` (0.002 TAO) or less —
  `validate_stake_transition`, `tao_equivalent > min_amount`, else `AmountTooLow` — and still takes the transaction fee;
  a partial fill under it leaves the TAO unstaked. The quote promised alpha all the same. It now returns
  `NOT_MEET_MIN_SWAP`, *"Amount must be at least X SYMBOL"*, in every direction: `DefaultMinStake` + 1 rao for TAO; for
  alpha the least amount whose simulated sale pays more, rounded up to 6 decimals. 3 tests. As the commit records:
  every named minimum quotes on mainnet; on testnet (spec 482) a move just under it failed `AmountTooLow`, one at it
  went through. The strict `>` matches the v478 read of 10-15 (EXT-12's source check).
- **[`f8d9546`](https://github.com/Koniverse/SubWallet-Monorepos/commit/f8d9546) — BE-18, 11:43.** Every Bittensor quote depended on one RPC. A chain in `API_MAPPINGS` can now list
  endpoints: `WsProvider` moves to the next each time the socket closes, and with more than one, a minute without a new
  head moves on too — a node that keeps its socket open but stops importing blocks would otherwise hold every request.
  Lists: Bittensor (Latent, Opentensor), Hydration (Dwellir, Galactic Council), Polkadot and both Asset Hubs (three
  each), chain-list providers of different operators; chains with no second live provider keep one. 5 tests. As the
  commit records, on mainnet: the backend's own Bittensor quote went through with the first endpoint dead, after a
  dropped socket and after a silent one (moved in 70 s), and Hydration's with its first endpoint dead; on healthy
  chains the longest gap between heads over 2 minutes was 12.3 s, so the watchdog stayed put.
- **The extension's side of BE-19 is already there:** PR #5113's Swap plan refuses an amount below the smallest leg the
  chain takes (`dce3b527e5`, `amountBelowMinimum`), with the same simulated-sale rule for alpha.
