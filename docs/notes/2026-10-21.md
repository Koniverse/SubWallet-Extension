## D. Proxy — validators' nominator counts per subnet (BE-4)

[`f10e80c`](https://github.com/Koniverse/Cloudflare-Workers/commit/f10e80c) *"Bittensor validators' nominator counts per subnet"*, `tunghp2002`, 04:42 UTC, on
`Cloudflare-Workers` branch `subwallet-api-proxier-dev` — the first change there since 08-10. +387 / −1 in
seven files, two of them tests (11 cases).

- **The route:** `/bittensor/subnet/validator/nominators/latest/v1?netuid=N` serves each validator's nominator count on a subnet, from TAO.com's public API
  (`api.tao.com/data/v1/subnets/{netuid}/validators/latest`, no key) — *"the one validator field the chain
  cannot give cheaply"*: the chain has no per-subnet nominator counter, and counting means scanning every
  stake entry of each hotkey.
- **Caching:** in the Durable Object, fresh for an hour — one fetch per subnet however many ask; if TAO.com
  fails, the old entry is served marked `stale`. The minute cron refreshes 10 subnets at a time that are
  missing or over 45 minutes old, over netuids 0 – 128. A 429 pauses every fetch for its `Retry-After`;
  netuids outside 0 – 255 are refused.
- **Direction as built:** SubWallet reads the rest of a subnet's validator list — stake, take — from the
  chain, and only the nominator count from the proxy. BE-4's 10-14 direction was the whole list through the
  proxy; this narrows it.
- **Live on the dev proxy, checked 05:29 UTC:** `external-services-dev.subwallet.app` answers — SN64: 82
  validators, refreshed 04:50; SN0: 14, refreshed 04:46. Production (`external-services.subwallet.app`)
  answers 404: not merged into `subwallet-api-proxier`, not deployed there.
- **Not used yet:** PR #5113 does not call the route; AC-11 (validator picker per subnet) is not started.
- **To check:** netuid 0 returns **14 validators**, against 174 funds with root stake on 10-14 — whether
  TAO.com's list is enough for root's picker. And TAO.com is a third-party source: its limits and uptime
  belong with BE-10.
